The Practice and Science of Drawing
Harold Speed, 1913
Public domain. Source: Project Gutenberg eBook 14264.

The painter’s equipment consists of a mind with which to think, eyes
with which to see, and a hand with which to record what the mind and
eye have determined. It is the training of this threefold equipment that
is the subject of this book.

The art of drawing is the interpretation of form on a flat surface. The
first thing the student has to learn is to see; the next, to think about
what he sees; and lastly, to express this seeing and thinking with a
pencil or brush.
